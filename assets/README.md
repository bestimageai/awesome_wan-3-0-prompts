## What the illustrations demonstrate

These frames establish a subject, material, environment, and composition for the linked creative brief. They do not demonstrate a complete timeline, audio, accurate lips, stable identity through motion, engineering compliance, or a validated model capability. A concept still may show one moment before or after a described action. It does not supply every reference, start frame, or end frame needed by a recipe.

The sports scene is a staged concept; the industrial scene is not a safety audit; the nature scene is not a wildlife record. Generated details should be inspected before using any frame in a production workflow.
