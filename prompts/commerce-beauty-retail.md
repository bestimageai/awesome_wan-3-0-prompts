# Commerce, beauty and retail

Twelve briefs for commerce, beauty and retail. All products are fictional. Use approved product images for geometry and substantiate real marketing claims independently. [Catalog](README.md)

## 01｜Refilling a soap bottle without changing the packaging

**Mode:** I2V · **Settings:** 12s, 16:9, sound on · **Inputs:** bottle-before start image; partly refilled end image

```text
Create a 12-second refill demonstration using the supplied images of one green transparent glass bottle, a detached pump on a tray, and an unmarked beige refill pouch. At 0–3s adult hands tilt the open pouch above the bottle mouth. At 3–9s a continuous narrow stream enters the opening and the level rises gradually. At 9–12s the hands straighten the pouch and stop pouring, leaving the pump on the tray. Use a fixed close three-quarter camera and soft shop daylight. Sound: liquid and pouch material, no speech. Preserve bottle volume, opening, hands, and pump. No pouring through a fitted pump, spills, invented capacity, labels, or environmental percentage claims.
```

**Adjust:** bottle and pouch colors. **Review:** the fluid enters the open mouth; the level cannot jump or exceed the reference.
