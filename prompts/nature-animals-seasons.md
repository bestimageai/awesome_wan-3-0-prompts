# Nature, animals and seasons

Twelve briefs for nature, animals and seasons. Generated nature imagery is illustrative, not wildlife evidence or animal-care guidance. Do not stage harmful interactions to obtain references. [Catalog](README.md)

## 01｜A kingfisher watches the rising tide

**Mode:** T2V · **Settings:** 12s, 16:9, sound on · **Inputs:** none

```text
Create a 12-second natural-history concept from a patient long-lens viewpoint. One kingfisher perches on a thin branch above a quiet wetland, with reeds behind and low dawn mist. At 0–4s it holds still except for breathing. At 4–8s a gentle incoming ripple reaches the reed stems; the bird turns its head once toward the water. At 8–12s it remains on the same branch while the ripple disperses. Keep the camera locked and distant. Sound: water, light wind, distant birds, no narration. Preserve plausible feathers, feet, scale, branch, and water level. No bait, diving sequence, extra bird, glowing plumage, text, or claim of an observed real event.
```

**Adjust:** dawn color and reed density. **Review:** the still and clip should agree on species appearance, perch, and natural scale.
