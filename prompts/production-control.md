# Production control and editing

Thirteen briefs for production control and editing. Every requested effect is a review target, not a guarantee. I2V below requires both start and end images on the documented bestimage.ai route. Editing examples keep source and output duration within the documented combined limit. [Catalog](README.md) · [API guide](../guides/bestimage-wan-3-api.md)

## 01｜A clean performance plate with visible feet

**Mode:** R2V · **Settings:** 10s, 16:9, sound off · **Inputs:** image 1 = consenting adult performer

```text
Image 1 defines the adult performer's face, proportions, charcoal clothing, and ivory scarf. Create a silent 10-second performance plate against a plain evenly lit blue studio background. At 0–3s the performer stands with feet visible and hands relaxed. At 3–7s they turn their shoulders a quarter turn and lift the scarf to chest height. At 7–10s they lower it and return to the starting stance. Use a locked camera and soft floor shadows. Keep fabric length, hands, identity, and background color constant. No camera movement, cropped feet, blue clothing, transparency, floating cloth, logos, or claim of a finished alpha matte.
```

**Adjust:** background color to avoid wardrobe overlap. **Review:** inspect edge separation and produce any actual matte in a dedicated workflow.
