# Industrial and manufacturing

Twelve visual briefs for industrial and manufacturing scenes. These are documentary concepts, mockups and review targets, not operating instructions, safety validation, or engineering evidence. Qualified personnel must review any use in training or product claims. [Catalog](README.md)

<a id="scene-01"></a>

## 01｜Sealed jars pass a guarded inspection station

**Mode:** T2V · **Settings:** 12s, 16:9, sound on · **Inputs:** none

```text
Create a fictional 12-second industrial documentary shot. Behind a transparent guard, a conveyor carries three unmarked sealed glass jars past a fixed camera inspection station. One separate jar already waits in a side review lane. At 0–4s establish the full guarded station and an adult observer outside it. At 4–8s the first jar passes the camera at constant speed. At 8–12s the second follows while the observer watches without reaching inside. Use a slow lateral camera move and low motor ambience, no speech. Preserve jar count, lids, guard, cables, and lane geometry. No invented pass rate, diagnostic overlay, moving sensor, loose wiring, missing lid, or claim of verified inspection accuracy.
```

**Adjust:** jar size supported by a reference. **Review:** all lids stay sealed and no hand enters the enclosure; concept art is not a compliance audit.
