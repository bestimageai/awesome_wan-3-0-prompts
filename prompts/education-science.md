# Education and science

Thirteen creative briefs for education and science. These are qualitative teaching visuals, not measurements, certified training, or experimental evidence. Have a subject specialist check the final output. [Catalog](README.md)

<a id="scene-01"></a>

## 01｜Shade moves across a model courtyard

**Mode:** T2V · **Settings:** 12s, 16:9, sound off · **Inputs:** none

```text
Create a silent 12-second teaching visualization of a miniature courtyard, one tree, two benches, and a fixed overhead lamp representing the sun. At 0–4s show the full model from a locked three-quarter camera. At 4–8s move the lamp slowly from left to right along a visible arc; the tree shadow changes direction across the paving. At 8–12s stop the lamp and hold on one shaded bench and one exposed bench. Keep the tree and benches fixed. Use realistic model materials and clearly simplified lighting. No temperature numbers, blue cooling waves, growing tree, moving buildings, or claim that shade alone determines a measured temperature.
```

**Adjust:** tree shape, lamp angle. **Review:** shadows move with the light; this is not a thermal simulation.

## 02｜One seed under a digital microscope

**Mode:** R2V · **Settings:** 10s, 16:9, sound off · **Inputs:** image 1 = authorized microscope image

```text
Use image 1 as the only source for the seed's actual shape and surface pattern. Create a silent 10-second illustrative microscope-camera move. At 0–3s show the full seed and neutral background. At 3–7s move closer to one visible ridge without changing orientation. At 7–10s hold the magnified region. Preserve every visible crack, edge, and texture; areas outside the source image should remain out of frame rather than be invented. Do not add cells, organisms, labels, scale bars, new colors, or a germination process. Maintain even illumination and a single smooth camera path. This is a visual enlargement concept, not additional microscopy data.
```

**Adjust:** crop target, camera speed. **Review:** compare the final frame with the source and reject invented features.

## 03｜A guarded workshop demonstration begins with a pause

**Mode:** T2V · **Settings:** 12s, 16:9, sound on · **Inputs:** none

```text
Create a fictional 12-second workshop orientation scene. A closed, idle desktop cutting machine sits behind a transparent guard, and an adult instructor stands beside it wearing appropriate workshop clothing. At 0–4s they keep both hands outside the enclosure. At 4–8s they point to the closed guard and then to a paper checklist on the adjacent bench. At 8–12s they place the checklist down and step back; the machine never starts. Camera stays wide enough to show feet, guard, and bench. Sound: paper and room tone, no speech. No laser beam, cutting settings, bypassed interlock, open enclosure, readable safety certification, or claim of a complete training procedure.
```

**Adjust:** room layout, machine color. **Review:** a qualified trainer must define real operating steps; this shot only establishes a cautious pause.

## 04｜A shadow crosses a classroom moon model

**Mode:** T2V · **Settings:** 12s, 16:9, sound off · **Inputs:** none

```text
Create a silent classroom model visualization with a lamp, an opaque blue sphere, and a smaller pale sphere on separate stands. At 0–4s a fixed side view establishes their aligned positions, with the lamp on the left. At 4–8s an adult hand moves the pale sphere slowly through the blue sphere's cast shadow. At 8–12s hold with part of the pale sphere in shadow. Keep the light source, stands, and sphere sizes constant. Show visible support rods so this reads as a demonstration, not space footage. No scale claims, orbital periods, glow from the blue sphere, changing shadow direction, or generated labels.
```

**Adjust:** sphere spacing within the approved demonstration. **Review:** confirm the geometry with a science educator; this model is not to scale.


## 07｜Water passing through a demonstration column

**Mode:** T2V · **Settings:** 12s, 16:9, sound on · **Inputs:** none

```text
Create a 12-second qualitative teaching model: a clear upper reservoir, a transparent column with three visibly distinct inert layers, and an empty collecting beaker. At 0–4s an adult hand pours a small amount of tinted demonstration water into the reservoir. At 4–8s the water level falls gradually and droplets appear below the column. At 8–12s show a small collected amount, with some tint remaining. Keep a fixed side camera and the layer order unchanged. Sound: pouring and drops, no narration. No drinking, purity claims, disappearing contamination, microscopic organisms, numerical flow rate, or construction instructions. The apparatus is an illustrative model, not a potable-water device.
```

**Adjust:** layer colors, backdrop. **Review:** fluid volumes should remain plausible; do not use the clip as proof of purification.

## 09｜A science presenter leaves space for interpretation

**Mode:** T2V · **Settings:** 12s, 16:9, sound on · **Inputs:** none

```text
Create a 12-second accessible-presentation layout concept. An adult science presenter stands on the left beside an unlit demonstration lamp; reserve the right third of the frame as an unobstructed neutral area for a real interpreter recording in post. At 0–4s the presenter looks at the lamp. At 4–9s they say, “We will watch where the shadow falls,” and gesture below shoulder height. At 9–12s they pause with hands relaxed. Keep a locked waist-up camera, clear speech, and quiet room tone. No generated sign language, subtitles, clutter in the reserved area, measurements, flashing light, or unsupported claim of accessibility compliance.
```

**Adjust:** presenter clothing, reserved side if the whole layout is redesigned. **Review:** use qualified interpretation and caption review for the final lesson.

## 10｜Keeping two demonstration trays separate

**Mode:** T2V · **Settings:** 10s, 16:9, sound on · **Inputs:** none

```text
Create a 10-second fictional kitchen-training staging shot. Two empty trays, one blue and one yellow, stand apart on a clean table, each with its own matching tongs. At 0–3s an adult instructor points to the two work areas. At 3–7s they lift the blue tongs and return them to the blue tray without crossing the gap. At 7–10s the camera slides slightly sideways, keeping both areas visible. Sound: tongs touching tray, room tone, no dialogue. Preserve colors, spacing, and tool count. No food handling, allergen-free claim, sterilization claim, ingredient labels, or implication that color coding alone prevents cross-contact.
```

**Adjust:** tray colors to match an approved training system. **Review:** a food-safety professional must supply the actual procedure.

```text
Create a silent 12-second transition in a clearly miniature nighttime street. Keep the supplied buildings, two streetlamps, road, and fixed camera identical. At 0–4s show the original unshielded lamp heads. At 4–8s adult hands fit the supplied simple covers above each lamp one at a time. No lux values, astronomical claims, energy savings, moving sun, or new fixtures. Make the covers' material and attachment visible.
```

**Adjust:** model color, approved cover design. **Review:** the clip illustrates direction, not measured sky brightness.

## 12｜A balance model settles after one added block

**Mode:** T2V · **Settings:** 10s, 16:9, sound on · **Inputs:** none

```text
Create a 10-second classroom mechanics concept. A simple two-pan balance is level, with one identical wooden block on each pan. At 0–3s show the whole apparatus from a fixed side camera. At 3–6s an adult hand places one additional matching block on the left pan and withdraws. At 6–10s the left pan lowers, the right rises, and the oscillation gradually settles. Sound: wood contact and a faint pivot creak, no speech. Keep three blocks total, equal arm lengths, and a stable base. No floating weights, changing block sizes, numerical mass readings, fingers holding the beam, or gravity-defying rebound.
```

**Adjust:** block color, background. **Review:** check force direction and object count before use in a lesson.

## 13｜A paper bridge bends under a small test weight

**Mode:** T2V · **Settings:** 12s, 16:9, sound on · **Inputs:** none

```text
Create a 12-second qualitative classroom test of one folded paper bridge spanning two low wooden supports. At 0–4s show the bridge and one small clay ball beside it. At 4–8s an adult hand places the ball gently at the center, then moves clear. At 8–12s the paper bends slightly under the ball and settles without collapsing. Use a fixed close side view showing both supports and the gap. Sound: soft contact and paper flex, no narration. Preserve the span, paper folds, clay ball size, and support positions. No load rating, architectural certification, invisible reinforcement, extra weights, or instantaneous material change.
```

**Adjust:** paper fold pattern, approved test object. **Review:** this is not evidence of a real structure's strength.
