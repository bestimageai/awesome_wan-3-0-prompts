# Professional business and public service

Thirteen directing briefs for business and public services. Use fictional data and approved assets. A generated interface is not an operational service or proof of accessibility. Set the listed duration and ratio in the platform, not only in text. [Catalog](README.md) · [Input limits](../guides/model-capabilities.md)

## 01｜From scattered notes to a shared workboard

**Mode:** T2V · **Settings:** 12s, 16:9, sound on · **Inputs:** none

```text
Create a 12-second product concept for a fictional planning workspace. An adult coordinator in a blue overshirt sits at a desk with three blank paper cards and a tablet showing three large empty columns. During 0–4s they arrange the cards; during 4–8s the camera slides right as they drag one abstract square into the middle tablet column; during 8–12s they place the corresponding paper card in the middle of the desk and pause. Sound: paper, one tap, quiet office tone; no narration. Keep the same hands, tablet, three cards, and column layout. No generated metrics, client logos, readable small text, floating UI, or success claims.
```

**Adjust:** card colors, desk material. **Review:** the paper and screen represent the same single change, without implying automatic synchronization.

## 02｜A bookbinding instructor shows one fold

```text
Image 1 supplies the adult instructor's face, gray curls, and green apron; image 2 supplies only the workbench. Make a 12-second vertical course preview. ## 03｜A two-language neighborhood welcome
```

**Mode:** T2V · **Settings:** 16s, 16:9, sound on · **Inputs:** none

```text
Create a fictional community-radio welcome with two adult hosts, one in rust red on the left and one in navy on the right. Keep a static medium two-shot. At 0–6s the left host says in Spanish, “Este espacio es para compartir las historias del barrio.” At 6–9s both pause and look toward each other. At 9–14s the right host says in English, “Bring a story, and we will listen.” Hold silently to 16s. Use natural voices, faint room tone, and no music. Keep microphones, faces, clothing, and seats fixed. Listeners keep their mouths closed. No flags, translations on screen, overlapping speech, or invented station branding.
```

**Adjust:** approved dialogue and host references. **Review:** speaker order and language remain distinct; allow enough time for revised lines.

## 04｜Finding the appointment desk

**Mode:** T2V · **Settings:** 12s, 16:9, sound on · **Inputs:** none

```text
Create a 12-second fictional community-center arrival scene, not medical advice. An adult visitor in a beige coat holds a blank appointment card. During 0–4s they enter a bright lobby and stop at a reception desk. During 4–8s the seated receptionist points toward a visible waiting area without taking the card. During 8–12s the visitor nods and walks two steps toward an empty chair. The camera pans gently with the visitor from the same side of the desk. Sound: footsteps and quiet room ambience, no dialogue. Preserve the lobby route, card, chairs, and identities. No patient details, diagnosis, clinical equipment, readable appointment information, or promises about waiting time.
```

**Adjust:** furniture color, visitor clothing. **Review:** the route is spatially continuous and no private data is shown.

## 06｜A returnable case is packed for its next trip

**Mode:** I2V · **Settings:** 12s, 1:1, sound on · **Inputs:** open-case start frame; closed-case end frame

```text
Animate a 12-second transition between the supplied frames of the same blue-gray returnable case. Two orange latches, one folded divider, and a blank label recess remain unchanged. At 0–4s adult hands fold the empty divider flat inside the case. At 4–8s they lower the lid from its actual hinge, keeping fingers outside the edge. At 8–12s they close the left latch, then the right, and withdraw. The camera remains fixed at a high three-quarter angle. Sound: fabric, lid, and two separate latch clicks. No contents appearing, instant folding, altered hinges, recycling symbols, shipping labels, or numerical environmental claims.
```

**Adjust:** case and latch colors. **Review:** the lid cannot close through the divider or hands.


## 08｜A shared battery room explained without numbers

**Mode:** T2V · **Settings:** 15s, 16:9, sound off · **Inputs:** none

```text
Create a silent 15-second architectural communication concept for a fictional community energy room. Show a small building model with roof panels, an enclosed equipment room, and three neighboring homes. At 0–5s a slow overhead camera establishes the entire model. At 5–10s one restrained amber line traces the designed route from roof to equipment room. At 10–15s three separate lines extend toward the homes and stop. Use a clearly stylized scale model, not documentary footage. Preserve building count, route positions, and the enclosed room. No exposed wiring instructions, power figures, guaranteed savings, certification badges, smoke, or claims that this is an engineered installation.
```

**Adjust:** model materials, approved route layout. **Review:** an engineer must validate any real diagram before publication.

## 12｜A quiet-space option at a workshop

**Mode:** T2V · **Settings:** 12s, 16:9, sound on · **Inputs:** none

```text
Create a 12-second workshop welcome concept. Two adult participants stand beside a studio table; one covers an ear briefly as a distant group talks. At 0–4s the host notices without touching them. At 4–8s the host gestures toward an open adjacent room with two empty chairs. At 8–12s the participant chooses to walk there while the host remains at the table. Use a slow lateral camera move that keeps the doorway visible. Sound softens naturally with distance, not sudden total silence. Preserve people, room plan, and chairs. No diagnosis, exaggerated distress, forced assistance, readable signage, or claims of universal accessibility.
```

**Adjust:** room design, background sound level. **Review:** the participant makes the choice and the route remains visible.

## 13｜A repair queue explained with physical tokens

**Mode:** T2V · **Settings:** 12s, 1:1, sound on · **Inputs:** none

```text
Create a 12-second public-service concept for a neighborhood repair desk. The counter has three shallow trays colored gray, amber, and green. At 0–4s a volunteer receives one blue wooden token from a visitor. At 4–8s they place it in the gray tray beside two existing tokens. At 8–12s they point to the other trays to explain the stages, without moving the new token further. Use a fixed overhead three-quarter camera. Sound: wood on wood, quiet room tone, no dialogue. Preserve token count, tray order, hands, and counter. No automatically completed repair, wait-time promise, personal information, readable labels, or floating icons.
```

**Adjust:** colors, tray material. **Review:** the submitted token stays in the first stage; add approved labels in post if required.
