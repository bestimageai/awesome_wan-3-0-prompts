# Architecture, hospitality and mobility

Thirteen briefs for architecture, hospitality and mobility. Use approved plans to define real spaces; video generation cannot validate dimensions, access standards, traffic safety, or structural performance. [Catalog](README.md)

```text
Use the three supplied views only to define the same small studio: entry at left, kitchenette ahead, sofa at right, window beyond. Create a 12-second walkthrough starting at the entrance. During 0–4s hold the doorway in frame; during 4–8s move forward slowly beside the kitchenette; during 8–12s stop before the sofa and pan slightly toward the window. Use a moderate field of view without stretching the room. Sound: two soft footsteps and room ambience, no speech. Preserve wall lengths relative to furniture, door positions, ceiling height, and fixtures. No extra rooms, impossible camera passage, square-meter claims, luxury additions, or readable listing text.
```

**Adjust:** approved furnishings, lighting. **Review:** compare each revealed wall against the supplied plans and photographs.
